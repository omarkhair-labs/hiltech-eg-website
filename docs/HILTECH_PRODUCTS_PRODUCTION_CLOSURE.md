## Final mobile typography correction

The full-size Product artifact review found a real mobile blocker in Product Intelligence:

- long titles such as **CABLE MANAGEMENT / DUCT SYSTEMS**
- and **PATCH CORDS & CONNECTIVITY**

were extending beyond the mobile grid and being visually clipped.

The cause was not a missing global overflow rule. The long word min-content width was expanding the mobile grid track while the page-level overflow clipping hid the failure.

The fix:
- forces Product Intelligence grid children to `min-width: 0`,
- lowers the mobile headline scale,
- allows safe title wrapping,
- constrains intro/summary text,
- adds browser QA that rejects horizontal text clipping rather than merely hiding it.

The corrected full-size captures show the titles, intro text, and strategic summaries fully contained.
